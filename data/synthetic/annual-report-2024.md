*Published 15 March 2025 (draft for the members' assembly). Reporting period: 1 January to 31 December 2024. All figures are from the association's own records.*

2024 was the year in which the "Schwarzes Bruch" peatland project passed its fifth year and the "Büro-Wattwächter" Green IT pilot started. The association (fictional) grew, and most of its budget went directly into projects.

## Projects

**Schwarzes Bruch.** The total rewetted area rose to 142 hectares by the end of 2024. Water-level loggers show that on the newest 31 hectares the water table stayed within 20 cm of the surface for most of the winter. We are not publishing a carbon saving figure because it has not been measured.

**Büro-Wattwächter.** In the pilot, 18 offices borrowed energy meters for two weeks. Participants reported finding standby loads and old devices as the main savings opportunities. We do not publish a pooled kWh saving because the measurement periods differed.

## Finances

Income came from membership fees, donations and one state grant for the peatland project. The budget share figures above are rounded to whole percent.

## Outlook

For 2025 the board plans to rewet further areas and to extend the Green IT pilot. The members' assembly will vote on the annual report on 29 March 2025.
