# Project "Büro-Wattwächter": Green IT for small offices

*Project page, last updated 9 September 2024*

"Büro-Wattwächter" is a campaign and advisory service by Moorfunke e.V. (fictional) for small offices, associations and one-person businesses that want to reduce the electricity use and electronic waste of their everyday IT.

## What we offer

- **Hardware check:** A volunteer visits the office or does a video call and lists the devices in use: laptops, monitors, printers, routers, servers. We note age, standby behaviour and whether repair or an upgrade of memory or storage would extend the life of a device.
- **Standby and settings:** Switchable power strips, sleep timers and sensible screen brightness are often the cheapest measures.
- **Cloud and hosting:** We explain in plain language what a data centre does, why utilisation matters, and what questions to ask a hosting provider about renewable electricity and efficiency. We do not recommend a specific company.
- **Procurement guide:** A two-page checklist for buying refurbished or repairable devices.

## How we measure

Participants may borrow a plug-in energy meter for two weeks. The pilot is just starting. We will report only what the participants measured themselves and will not extrapolate to other offices.

## Limits

Our advice is general. We do not audit data centres, and we cannot verify the claims of hardware or cloud providers. For general background on the energy use of cloud services and data centres, see the cited external sources in the knowledge base (ec-cloud-energy-2020, dewiki-rechenzentrum); we make no claims of our own about sector-wide figures.

## Participation

The service is free for members and available for a small donation for non-members. Volunteers are trained in one evening session.
